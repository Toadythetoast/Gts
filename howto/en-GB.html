The GTS is a building located in Jubilife City that allows you to trade Pokémon with other Trainers all across the world in Pokémon Diamond and Pokémon Pearl. You can search for Pokémon available for trade from the Pokémon you have registered in your Pokédex.
This Pokémon GTS website is where you can use your PC to check out all of the trades going on around the world using the high-tech "Geonet" globe.
 
On the first screen select your language. There are 7 languages to choose from. After you get used to this website, why don't you try viewing the site in different languages? This is the main screen of the Pokémon GTS website!
This is the high-tech "Geonet" globe from the game recreated on your PC. Here you can check out information on trades that happen on the GTS in Pokémon Diamond and Pokémon Pearl for the Nintendo DS. Go ahead and touch the globe. You can see many different countries by rotating and zooming.
If you see a Pokémon you're interested in, try clicking on it!
You'll be able to view all sorts of information, such as:
The Pokémon's height and weight, as well as its name in different languages.
Top 10 Pokémon that are traded most often for this Pokémon.
If you want to trade for this Pokémon, check what other players are trading for it here!
Top 10 Pokémon the people who want this Pokémon are depositing.
If you deposit this Pokémon, your Pokémon may be traded with any of the Pokémon listed here!
Information about recently deposited Pokémon on the GTS.
If you are looking for a specific Pokémon, use the "Pokémon Search" function. You can search for any Pokémon by their Pokédex number.

Name of the country

You can bookmark a Pokémon or country by clicking on the "Add to bookmark" button found on the Pokémon/country information screen. And if you click on the "See Bookmark" button, you can see Pokémon you previously bookmarked and even write comments.
