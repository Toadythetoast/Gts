Trade Pokémon with fans from around the world!

By connecting your Pokémon Diamond and Pokémon Pearl software for your Nintendo DS to the Internet using the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection, you can trade Pokémon or engage in battle play with people around the world.

Deposit your Pokémon on the GTS and trade Pokémon with fans from around the world.

You might even be able to obtain Pokémon that you need by using the easy, safe and free Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection!

You can check out the Pokémon GTS site (http://www.pokemon-gts.net) to see how Trainers around the world are trading Pokémon!
 
1. What is the source of the date and time information that appears on the site, such as the date and time of a Pokémon trade?
The date and time information that appears on the "Pokémon GTS" site is based on Greenwich Mean Time.
2. When is the data updated?
Data is updated every day around 01:30 (Greenwich Mean Time), and would be the data from the previous day (GMT).

MINIMUM SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:

1. Internet Brower:
Pokémon GTS runs properly on the following browsers (please note that there is a chance this site cannot be viewed using a browser not listed below):
Windows Internet Explorer 6.0 SP2 and above
Macintosh Safari 1.3 and above
Firefox 2.0 and above
Opera 9.2 and above

2. JavaScript, StyleSheet (CSS)
JavaScript or StyleSheet (CSS) is used on every page of Pokémon GTS. Please set your browser to allow for the use of JavaScript and StyleSheet (CSS).

3. Plug-in
The following plug-in is necessary to view Pokémon GTS.
Macromedia® Flash™ Player Ver 9,0,45,0 and above
* Administrator's authority is necessary when updating the Flash Player. Please consult a parent or guardian before updating if you are 16 years old or younger.
5. Can we view the site from the Wii console?
Sorry. This site cannot be viewed using the Wii console.
6. The message "Click here for Flash Player Installation" still appears after installing Flash Player.
This message appears because the Flash Player installation is not completed on your PC. You might need to restart your computer, or your security software might be the reason for the failed installation. Another reason may be that your PC does not meet the minimum requirements.
