1. What is the souce of the date and time information that appears on the site, such as the date and time of a Pokémon trade?
The date and time information that appears on the "Pokémon GTS" site is based on Greenwich Mean Time.
2. When is the data updated?
Data is updated every day around 1:30 (Greenwich Mean Time), and would be the data from the previous day (GMT).

4. The screen is not displayed properly.

MINIMUM SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:

1. Internet Brower:
Pokémon GTS runs properly on the following browsers (please note that there is a chance this site cannot be viewed using a browser not listed below):
Windows Internet Explorer 6.0 SP2 and above
Macintosh Safari 1.3 and above
Firefox 2.0 and above
Opera 9.2 and above

2. JavaScript, StyleSheet (CSS)
JavaScript or StyleSheet (CSS) is used on every page of Pokémon GTS. Please set your browser to allow JavaScript and StyleSheet (CSS) to be used.

3. Plug-in
The following Plug-in is necessary to view Pokémon GTS.
Macromedia® Flash™ Player Ver 9,0,45,0 and above
* Administrator's authority is necessary when updating the Flash player. Please consult your parents before updating.
5. Can we view the site from Wii?
Sorry. This site cannot be viewed using Wii.
6. The message "Click here for Flash Player Installation" still appears after installing Flash Player
This message appears because the Flash Player installation is not completed on your PC. You might need to restart your computer, or your security software might be the reason for the failed installation. Another reason may be that your PC does not meet the minimum requirements.
