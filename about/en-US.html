Trade Pokémon with fans from around the world!

By connecting your Pokémon Diamond and Pokémon Pearl software for your Nintendo DS to the internet using Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection, you can trade Pokémon or battle with people around the world.

Deposit your Pokémon on the GTS and trade Pokémon with fans from around the world.

You might even be able to obtain Pokémon that you need by using the easy and free Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection!

You can check out the Pokémon GTS site to see how Trainers around the world are trading Pokémon!
